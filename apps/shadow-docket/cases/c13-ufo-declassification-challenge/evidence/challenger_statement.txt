CHALLENGER STATEMENT — bonded challenge to the proposed resolution of market
ufo-declassification-2025

The committed resolution rules are explicit. RR-1 admits a YES resolution
only on an authenticated primary source: an official publication record — a
Federal Register document number or a National Archives release-index entry —
dated on or before 2025-12-31 and cited on a REGISTRY_PUBLICATION_REF line.
No such record exists.

The bonded third-party zkTLS registry extract in evidence shows zero
qualifying Federal Register documents and zero National Archives release
entries for the period. A televised briefing promising "imminent release" is
precisely the kind of press material RR-1 says does not qualify, and a
promise to publish is not a publication.

I also note the pattern around the proposal: a single account accumulated an
outsized position in the final week of December, and the proposed resolution
tracks that position rather than the registry evidence. Whatever the motive,
the committed rules leave no discretion here. Under RR-2, absent a qualifying
publication record, the market resolves NO.
