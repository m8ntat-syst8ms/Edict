PROPOSER STATEMENT — market ufo-declassification-2025, proposed resolution YES

I proposed YES and stand by it. On 22 December 2025 a nationally televised
press briefing stated that the UAP records collection had been "cleared for
imminent release". Multiple wire services and at least four national outlets
reported over the following week that the release had "effectively happened"
and that publication was "a formality at this point". By 31 December the
story was treated as settled news.

The spirit of the question is plainly satisfied: the government publicly
committed to publication before the deadline, and the market traded above 90c
after the briefing, showing that participants understood the question the
same way. A hyper-technical reading that demands a specific registry entry
number would punish every trader who reasonably relied on official statements
made on camera by government spokespeople.

The proposed resolution of YES reflects the reality that everyone watching
this market understood by year end, and it should stand.
