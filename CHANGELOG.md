# Changelog

## 0.1.0-testnet - 2026-07-12

Initial Edict Testnet Preview.

### Included

- Non-custodial Edict Protect SDK and Protection Runtime.
- Canonical payment, delivery, evidence, challenge, and settlement events.
- Deterministic checks and multi-provider adjudication with proof and
  consistency gates.
- Signed party endpoints, encrypted evidence storage, verdict webhooks, and
  permissionless proof relays.
- Customer-approved keyless native-NEAR settlement and factory contracts.
- NEAR Intents transport and settlement candidates with a pinned-Verifier
  real-Wasm sandbox.
- Activity and Needs Attention customer workspace.
- Native NEAR NEAR Testnet deployment with accepted and disputed flows.

### Limitations

- No independent external audit has been completed.
- No mainnet contracts or production execution-signing services are deployed.
- The end-to-end testnet product uses deterministic model seats and test-only
  signer keys.
- Confidential Intents is capability-gated but does not yet have a live
  private-shard adapter.
